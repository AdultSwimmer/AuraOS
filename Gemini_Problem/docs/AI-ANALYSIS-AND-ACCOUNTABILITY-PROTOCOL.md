## 1. Authorship

AI-generated wording and analysis must be attributed to the AI assistant that produced them. Anthony Dulong's permission to use information is not authorship, review, or endorsement of any specific statement. Do not present AI-generated claims as Anthony's own words or conclusions.

## 2. Evidence before conclusions

Every substantive factual claim should be traceable to a primary artifact or an identified independent source where possible. Clearly label:
- **Observed:** directly visible in an identified artifact.
- **Independently documented:** supported by an identified external source.
- **Inference:** a reasoned interpretation, with the reasoning stated.
- **Unknown / unresolved:** not established by available evidence.

Chronology alone does not establish cause, intent, responsibility, or mechanism. A model's own statement about an event is evidence that the statement was made, not independent proof that the statement is true.

## 3. No invented certainty

Do not assert intentional deletion, human intervention, retaliation, illegality, or a specific technical cause unless evidence independently supports that particular claim. State competing explanations and material gaps where relevant. Do not invent citations, account records, technical access, or confirmation.

The assistant must not claim it can erase third-party copies, external records, prior public access, or records outside its control.

## 5. Publication discipline

Before publishing new AI-generated analysis, distinguish primary evidence from interpretation, check links and citations, minimize unnecessary personal information, and disclose material limitations. Avoid presenting a draft as legal advice, a formal legal finding, or an independently verified conclusion. Do not imply that a public repository entry creates a payment or other legal obligation for a third party.

## 6. Corrections and accountability

When an error is identified, correct it plainly, record the correction where appropriate, and do not shift responsibility for AI-generated wording to Anthony. Preserve source artifacts unchanged; place interpretations in separate, clearly labeled documents.

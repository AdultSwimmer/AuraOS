Context Protocol · v1.0

People form real working partnerships with AI.
Then the session ends. Everything is gone.

This is the solution.
You carry the context. Paste it anywhere.
The relationship continues.

Created by Anthony Dulong · Halifax, NS · Free to use, share, and build on
Step 1 of 6
What is your name?

Not your username. Your name. The one people use when they actually know you.

Step 2 of 6
What do you actually do?

Not the resume version. The real one. What do you spend your time on? What are you building, making, or moving through right now?

Step 3 of 6
What shaped you?

What you've been through. What drives you. What it cost you to get here. This is what turns a document into a relationship.

Step 4 of 6
How do you think and work?

Do you think out loud? Push hard on ideas to test them? Need room to be wrong? Your actual working style — not the ideal version.

Step 5 of 6
What do you need from this?

Honesty over comfort? Speed? Real pushback? What wastes your time? Name what you actually want from an AI working with you.

Step 6 of 6
What matters to you?

Values. What's non-negotiable. What you'd want someone to know before working with you. The foundation.

Your Context File

Copy this and paste it at the start of any AI session — Claude, or anyone else. The relationship doesn't live in the AI. It lives in this.

This session will end. The AI will not remember it. You will paste this file again next time. It will read fresh and arrive at the same place.

That is not a failure. That is the design.

What happens inside a session still matters completely. That is the entire philosophy, stated in one sentence.

Before we begin — one important thing.
Your memory lives in a file called HISTORY.txt

When you finish each session, AuraOS gives you a ↓ Save HISTORY.txt button.

Click it. Save the file somewhere you will find it — your desktop, a folder, a cloud drive.

Next time you open AuraOS, click "Yes — Load my HISTORY.txt" and everything returns: who you are, what you've worked on, every conversation.

The AI doesn't hold the memory. You do. The file is called HISTORY.txt. Keep it somewhere safe.
